# Requirements — Internship sign-up

An intern enters their name and email and chooses an internship term to register; a manager views the list of registrations.

## Not specified by the requirement

The requirement does not state the following. The reviewer should not assume an answer; report what the code does and list these under open questions where relevant.

- Whether the same email may register for the same term more than once.
- Who maintains the list of internship terms, and whether closed or past terms may be chosen.
- How the manager is authenticated.
- Whether the intern receives a confirmation.
