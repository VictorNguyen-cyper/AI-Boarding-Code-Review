# Test cases — Internship sign-up

Task **T4** (Tier 2 — systematic behavior testing). Generated by AI from the requirement only, before any code was written. The *Actual result* and *Pass?* columns are filled in by a human while trying the feature.

---

## Requirements

> An intern enters their name and email and chooses an internship term to register; a manager views the list of registrations.

## Preconditions

- Accounts: 1 manager account; interns do not need an account (the requirement does not mention intern login).
- Required data: at least 2 internship terms available to choose from; one state with 0 registrations and one with ≥ 200 registrations.
- How to simulate a slow network: DevTools → Network → Slow 3G.
- Two browsers (or one normal + one private window): one for the intern, one for the manager.

## Open questions about the requirement

The requirement does not say the following. Note how the built feature behaves, and confirm with your mentor whether it is right:

- Can the same email register for the same term twice? For two different terms?
- Who creates the list of terms, and are past terms still selectable?
- How does the manager log in, and can an intern open the manager's list?
- Does the intern see a confirmation? Is an email sent?
